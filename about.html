About Ferns & Petals

Ferns & Petals started in 2015 as a small family owned flower shop. What began as a tiny stall with a handful of flower buckets has grown into a shop that people trust for weddings, birthdays and everyday gifts.

We believe flowers are more than a decoration, they are a way to say things that are sometimes hard to say out loud. That is why we put so much care into every bouquet that leaves our shop.

All of our flowers are sourced from local growers so that they reach you as fresh as possible. We also try to keep our prices fair so that sending flowers does not have to be expensive.
